## Observed problem and change

A request to perform QA Review for a filtered ticket column was handled as an
evidence-only audit. Candidate source and local checks were reviewed, but the
installed QA route and evaluator were skipped because end-to-end fixtures were
missing. The missing fixtures should have kept required scenarios incomplete;
they did not prevent a bounded evaluator result. This change makes that decision
explicit in the Temari entry, QA writer, and ticket acceptance playbook.

The trial uses synthetic wording and the same fixed evidence distinctions for
both versions. No private ticket text, account, or customer data is retained.

## Route-announcement follow-up

For a user who opted into route visibility, the intended first update names the
inferred request type, the requested ticket scope, the selected playbook or unavailable capability,
and admission state. In the five trial prompts above, full QA names ticket
acceptance and its pending/blocked evaluator route; draft-only work names the
writer route; sprint planning and triage disclose that their playbooks are still
design proposals in this public artifact. A changed route requires a brief
correction with its reason. This is a source contract; no independent model replay
has established announcement behavior yet.
