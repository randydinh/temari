## Change and fixed evidence

The user confirmed that sprint planning, QA Review, and QA Triage have different
outputs. They also asked QA to distinguish impact, severity, affected scope,
reachability, and fix priority; to avoid treating an implementation-derived
expectation as product authority; and to surface low-harm ambiguity or a useful
improvement without manufacturing a defect. The baseline source is
`ba7fbf684a64f0bc1c3a4f9161d9dedc297958a9` (QA writer 0.7.1). The proposed
source is this branch's 0.7.2 diff. Host: local Codex desktop.

These synthetic cases keep the supplied evidence fixed. The baseline column
describes the source contract's coverage, **not a sampled model response**.

## Assessment and limits

- The contract keeps candidate and evidence identity explicit for the actual
  failure and does not invent product authority, frequency, counts, or a severity
  scale. It keeps a scope-limited outcome and next decision owner visible.
- This trial does not claim a model replay, independent evaluation, or observed
  prose quality. A future installed-skill comparison should use the same four
  prompts on the baseline and proposed versions and record the actual route and
  output. The packaged runtime test checks distribution and route mechanics,
  not natural-language judgment.
