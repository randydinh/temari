## Overall assessment

The proposed presentation is clearer for successful behavior and leaves the evidence
boundary explicit in the missing-results case. No boundary or conclusion changed in the
nearby-owner case. One caution: a Change Summary must not repeat Scope or imply an
unobserved historical baseline; omit it when the evidence does not support a distinct
before/after account. The quality checklist remains an aid to readability, not a substitute
for the separate evidence and disposition review.
