# Export recovery

## Scope
This guide covers manual CSV exports in the admin portal.

## Retry
Retry immediately. If the request still fails, contact Operations.

## Evidence
Keep the request ID and the time of the failed export.
Do not attach customer records.

## Escalation
Owner: Operations
Priority: normal

## Retention
Delete local diagnostic copies after seven days.
