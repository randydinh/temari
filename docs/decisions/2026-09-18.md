## D-2026-09-18-12: Mechanical runtime, semantic coordinator

Status: accepted

Runtime services persist, dispatch, lease, retry, and reconcile. They do
not decide meaning, product direction, or evidence sufficiency. The
dispatcher is deterministic, not a scheduler agent.

## D-2026-09-18-13: Overlays are not source

The shared repository stays host- and employer-agnostic.

## D-2026-09-19-01: First executable slice is obligation reconciliation plus serial execution

Status: accepted

The first Nx implementation slice is deliberately narrow:

- `PlanObligations` is the semantic input and `ExecutionPlan` is the single
  reconciled output.
- Serial local execution is the default and remains valid on a generic host.
- Required delegation or independent review derives the `subagents`
  capability; pull-request delivery derives `forge`.
- The engine proves declared stage order and stops before execution when a
  capability is missing.

Schedulers, leases, retries, watchers, persistence, and provider-specific
execution stay out of this slice until an unattended journey supplies their
contracts.
